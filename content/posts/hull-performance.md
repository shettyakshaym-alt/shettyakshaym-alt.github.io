## The Hull Is Always Working

Hull performance is one of the simplest ways to save fuel at sea. A clean, well-maintained hull reduces resistance and helps the vessel move through water with less power.

## What Affects Hull Performance

- Fouling from marine growth
- Surface roughness
- Hull damage or coating wear
- Draft and trim condition
- Speed profile over time

## Why It Matters

Even a small increase in resistance can create a large fuel penalty on long voyages. That is why hull cleaning, coating selection, and performance monitoring are so important.

## Practical Takeaway

If you want lower fuel use and better emissions, hull care is not optional — it is a core operating strategy.
