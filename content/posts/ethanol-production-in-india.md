## Why Ethanol Matters

Ethanol is becoming an important part of India’s energy transition. It supports fuel blending, reduces crude import dependence, and creates new value for agricultural supply chains.

## Production Sources

India produces ethanol from:

- Sugarcane molasses
- Broken grains
- Surplus rice and maize
- Other biomass feedstocks

## What Is Driving Growth

- Government blending targets
- Better policy support
- Rising refinery and distribution demand
- Rural income support for farmers

## Shipping Angle

For maritime readers, ethanol matters because biofuel markets influence future marine fuel supply chains. Strong domestic production can help India become a larger energy hub.
