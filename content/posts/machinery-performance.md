## Why Machinery Performance Matters

A ship can have the best route plan in the world, but if machinery is inefficient, fuel is wasted. Engine performance, auxiliary systems, and operating practices all affect cost and emissions.

## Key Areas to Watch

- Main engine load and specific fuel consumption
- Lubrication condition and oil analysis
- Cooling system efficiency
- Fuel injection and combustion quality
- Auxiliaries such as pumps, compressors, and generators

## Common Mistakes

Many vessels lose efficiency because small issues are ignored:

- Fouled filters
- Poor maintenance intervals
- Incorrect tuning
- Running equipment at bad load levels

## Simple Rule

Measure more, guess less. Trend data from machinery helps detect problems early and keeps fuel use under control.
