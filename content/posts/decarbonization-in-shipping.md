## Shipping Must Change

Shipping is one of the hardest sectors to decarbonize, but it is also one of the most important. The industry depends on heavy fuel oil today, yet pressure is rising from regulators, customers, and financiers to cut emissions quickly.

## What Actually Helps

- Cleaner fuels like methanol, ammonia, LNG, and biofuels
- Better voyage planning and slower steaming
- Energy-saving devices and smart operations
- Better hull and machinery maintenance

## The Real Challenge

The biggest barrier is not awareness. It is execution. New fuels need ships, engines, ports, and supply chains to work together. That means shipping companies must start testing now, not wait for a perfect solution.

## Bottom Line

Decarbonization in shipping is no longer a future topic. It is a present-day business decision.
